# Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA)
## Website Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions
**Document ID**: IIMA-WEB-PRIVACY-2024  
**Status**: Approved  
**Effective Date**: 2024-01-01  
**Category**: Institutional Digital Governance  

### 1. Introduction and Scope
This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use govern the collection, processing, and handling of information collected through the official website of the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (`https://www.iima.ac.in`) and associated public academic portals.

### 2. Information Collection and Processing
1. **Personal Information**: The Institute collects personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and academic qualifications) only when voluntarily submitted by users through application portals, event registrations, or contact forms.
2. **Automated Session Data**: When visitors access the website, web servers automatically log domain names, IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and page visitation timestamps. This information is utilized solely for statistical aggregation, performance monitoring, and server security analysis.
3. **Cookies**: The website utilizes essential session cookies to preserve user navigation preferences and authentication tokens across secure forms. Cookies do not extract data from the user's hard drive.

### 3. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing
1. IIMA does not rent, sell, or disclose personal data collected on this website to external third-party commercial organizations.
2. Data may only be shared with governmental, regulatory, or judicial bodies where explicitly mandated under Indian law or statutory court orders.

Unauthorized downloading, republication, commercial redistribution, or reproduction without prior written authorization from the Competent Authority is strictly prohibited.

### 5. Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability
While every effort is made to maintain accurate and up-to-date information, the Institute accepts no liability for errors or omissions. Official academic curricula, admissions guidelines, and administrative regulations published in gazetted orders or signed institutional notifications shall supersede web-published summaries in all formal inquiries.
