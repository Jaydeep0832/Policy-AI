# Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad
## Executive HR Advisory Committee Memorandum
**Document ID**: IIMA-MEMO-HR-2026-04  
**Date**: 15 May 2026  
**Subject**: Special Addendum on Leave Accumulation Ceilings for Executive & Senior Administrative Cadres  
**Status**: Approved (Internal Governance Memo)  
**Effective Date**: 2026-05-15  

### Section 1: Scope & Applicability
This memorandum applies strictly to Senior Administrative Officers (Level 11 and above) and Executive Cadre Staff at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad.

### Section 2: Modified Accumulation Limits for Earned Leave (EL)
1. Notwithstanding Section 5.2.2 of the Staff HR Policy Manual 2026 (which provides an institutional general ceiling of 300 days), the maximum permissible ceiling on the accumulation of Earned Leave for Executive Cadre Staff covered under this Memorandum shall be restricted to **180 days**.
2. Any Earned Leave standing to the credit of an Executive Cadre employee in excess of 180 days at the close of the calendar year (31st December) shall be automatically converted to cash encashment up to a maximum of 15 days, with the remainder lapsing immediately.
3. This measure is instituted to mitigate accumulated balance sheet liabilities and encourage regular executive rest and recuperation.

### Section 3: Legal Hierarchy & Administrative Precedence
In the event of an interpretive conflict between this Departmental Addendum and the Institute's Gazetted Regulations, the statutory Gazetted Regulations shall supersede. For matters concerning general non-executive staff, the Master Staff HR Policy Manual 2026 remains the sole authoritative baseline.
