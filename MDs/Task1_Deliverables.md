# Task-1: Internal Policy Question-Answering Assistant - Objective and Deliverables

## Objective
The primary objective of Task-1 is to develop an AI-powered assistant capable of answering employee questions based **exclusively** on a provided set of policy documents. The assistant must accurately retrieve information, cite specific source documents and sections, and intelligently handle various edge cases such as conflicting policies, outdated/draft documents, and unanswerable questions. 

## Final Deliverables
To successfully complete Task-1, the following deliverables are required:

1. **Working Application or API**: The functional implementation of the QA assistant.
2. **Source Code and Instructions**: The complete codebase accompanied by clear setup and execution instructions.
3. **Evaluation Dataset**: A curated small set of test questions covering three specific scenarios:
   - Answerable questions.
   - Unanswerable questions (where information is missing from the documents).
   - Questions involving conflicting policies.
4. **Technical Write-up**: A concise explanation detailing:
   - The overall system architecture.
   - The document chunking strategy.
   - The retrieval approach.
   - The evaluation methodology used.
5. **Limitations Analysis**: Documented examples of incorrect answers generated by the system and any observed limitations of the current implementation.
